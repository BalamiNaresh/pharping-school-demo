Our Facilities

State-of-the-art civil engineering infrastructure designed for hands-on technical learning.

Infrastructure

World-Class Facilities

Pharping Secondary School is equipped with modern infrastructure to deliver practical, industry-standard civil engineering education.

Civil Engineering Lab

Fully equipped lab for practical sessions including material testing, water supply experiments, and structural model building.

AutoCAD Computer Lab

Dedicated computer lab with AutoCAD software for civil drawing, land measurement, and design projects.

Survey Equipment

Total stations, theodolites, and leveling instruments for engineering survey practicals in and around Pharping.

Classrooms

Spacious, well-ventilated classrooms with teaching aids, whiteboards, and a conducive learning atmosphere.

Workshop

Hands-on construction workshop for wiring, plumbing, masonry, and other construction technology practicals.

Drawing Room

Dedicated drawing room for manual drafting and technical drawing practicals, fundamental to civil engineering training.

Campus Environment

Located in the heart of historical Pharping Bazaar, next to Panjal Ganesh — serene, safe, and ideal for focused study.

On-the-Job Training (OJT)

Partnerships with local construction companies for real-world OJT placements, giving students industry exposure.

Science Lab

Fully stocked science laboratory supporting general science subjects alongside the Civil Engineering technical stream.

Technical Education

Built for Engineers

Technical education in Civil Engineering at Pharping Secondary School provides real-world skills: land measurement, AutoCAD drawing, wiring, plumbing, and construction supervision.

These skills generate technical manpower, provide opportunities for self-employment through own firms and companies, and open doors to international education and employment.
