Admissions & Fees

Join Nepal's leading Civil Engineering technical stream. Applications open for Batch 2082 B.S.

Eligibility

Who Can Apply?

Passed Class 8

Must have passed Grade 8 with a minimum C grade in each subject.

Entrance Examination

Must score 50% or above in the entrance examination conducted by the school.

Interest in Technical Education

Students motivated to pursue civil engineering, construction, or related technical fields.

Documents

Required Documents

  • 1 Grade 8 marksheet (original + copy)
  • 2 Character certificate from previous school
  • 3 Transfer certificate
  • 4 Birth certificate (copy)
  • 5 Citizenship of parent/guardian (copy)
  • 6 Recent passport-size photographs (4 copies)
  • 7 Completed application form (available at school office)
Note: Contact the school office for exact documents and any updates. Visit us at Dakshinkali-06, Pharping or call +977-1-4710147.

Process

Admission Steps

1

Visit / Contact School

Visit us in person or call +977-1-4710147 to express your interest and collect the admission form.

2

Submit Application Form

Complete and submit the application form with all required documents to the school office.

3

Appear in Entrance Exam

Sit for the school's entrance examination. Minimum 50% score required to qualify.

4

Merit List & Selection

A merit list is published based on entrance exam scores. Selected students are notified.

5

Fee Payment & Enrollment

Pay the admission fee, confirm enrollment, collect your schedule, and join the program.
