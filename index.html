About the School

A Legacy of Quality Education

Pharping Secondary School was established in 1976 B.S. as a modern and dynamic education centre. Situated in the heart of historical Pharping Bazaar, next to Panjal Ganesh, the school has grown into one of the region's most respected academic institutions.

Approximately 592 students are currently enrolled, including 100 students in the flagship Civil Engineering (9–12) technical stream. Three batches have already appeared in the +2 board examination with a 100% pass record.

Why Choose Us

Civil Engineering Excellence

A unique technical-vocational program designed by the Government of Nepal to produce skilled, job-ready engineers from Grade 9.

📐

AutoCAD & Drawing

Hands-on AutoCAD training for civil drawing, land measurement, and design concepts used in real construction environments.

Plumbing & Wiring

Academics

Course Structure

A comprehensive curriculum blending theoretical foundations with practical application.

Foundation Year (Class 9)

  • General Subjects
  • Computer and Drawing
  • Water Supply and Sanitary Engineering
  • Construction Technology and Workshop
  • Water Resource Engineering

Applied Skills Year (Class 10)

  • General Subjects
  • Construction and Drawing
  • Highway Engineering
  • Engineering Survey-I
  • Estimation, Costing and Supervision-I

Advanced Technical Year (Class 11)

  • General Subjects
  • Geo Technical Engineering
  • Estimation, Costing and Supervision-II
  • Engineering Survey-II
  • Applied Mechanics

Professional Capstone Year (Class 12)

  • General Subjects
  • Mechanics of Structure
  • Fluid Mechanics
  • RCC Structure
  • Construction Management
